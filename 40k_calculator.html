How to read this: Each of the 10,000 runs rolls every die individually through the full attack sequence — attacks, hits, wounds, saves, damage, and FNP. The histogram shows how often each total damage value occurred. A tall bar means that outcome is common; a wide spread means the weapon is swingy.
